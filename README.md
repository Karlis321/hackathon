### The three 2026 challenges

1. **AS "Sadales tīkls": second life for grid materials** (this repository). Cable offcuts, wooden poles and concrete poles. The website also frames it as a tool, decision model or process that picks the best next use for surplus material (reuse, refurbishment, sale, donation or recycling), especially matching cable offcuts to new projects.
2. **Latvijas Zaļais punkts: packaging recycling emissions.** A calculator or data tool for the CO₂-equivalent emissions of recycling plastic packaging, especially LDPE film. It compares at least two recycling scenarios and the savings of recycled versus virgin material, follows GHG Protocol and LCA principles, and can be adapted to cardboard and glass.
3. **EIT Urban Mobility Hub: EV batteries.** Second life and longer lifespan for electric vehicle batteries, and less battery waste.

## Our challenge: Sadales tīkls

> How can we give a second life to cable offcuts and dismantled wooden or concrete
> power-line poles, finding a new, safe and economically viable use that also
> reduces resource consumption and environmental impact?

Teams pick **one** of three material groups:

| Group | Current situation | What's needed |
|---|---|---|
| **A. Cable offcuts** | Offcuts of usable length are already reused in the grid. Very short pieces are not worth it (jointing labour, extra materials, joint reliability). | A use outside the grid, or a way to match offcuts to uses by cable type, length and condition. |
| **B. Wooden poles** | Already reused in power lines (444 poles in 2026 so far). Some cannot be reused. | Uses for whole poles or parts, accounting for impregnation chemicals and their restrictions; safe treatment or recycling routes. |
| **C. Concrete poles** | Not reused: they almost always break and crumble during dismantling. Reinforced-concrete sub-poles *are* reused to extend wooden pole life. | A use for broken poles, parts or crushed material that cuts handling cost and replaces new materials. |

Possible users and partners: Sadales tīkls technical specialists, project managers, warehouse and logistics staff, and construction, recycling and other companies. Solutions must be safe and meet regulations, with particular care for wood impregnation chemicals, the condition of concrete structures, and whether a cable fits its intended use.

## Evaluation criteria

The organisers have not published a formal scoring rubric. What we know:

**From the event FAQ:** the jury judges each solution's **potential for further development** and its **innovativeness**.

**From the Sadales tīkls brief, the jury wants to see:**

1. **A clearly defined solution**: how it works, who it is for, what problem it solves.
2. **Practical implementation**: the key technical and safety conditions.
3. **A simplified economic case** compared with today's material handling.
4. **Benefits**: resources saved, costs reduced, environmental impact.
5. **Next steps** for validation or a pilot.

> "An interesting idea alone is not enough": the idea must be feasible and its value **backed by numbers**.
> A finished product is not required in three days.

Accepted forms: digital tool, new product, circularity or logistics solution, business model, or another practical idea.

### Stock list overview

- **LV indoor/installation copper cable**: (N)YM, H07V-K/U, YSLY, YSLYCY control cable (100 m drums)
- **LV power cable 0.6/1 kV**: NYY-J (Cu), AXMK / AXMK-PE / AXPK-PE (Al, 4×16 to 4×240)
- **MV cable 12/20–24 kV** (mostly Al): AHXAMK-W, AHXCMK-WTC, AXLJ, AXLJ-F TT, AXLJ-TTCL, AXCEL, AXQJ-TT, TSLE
- **Overhead cable**: AMKA in several sizes, plus dismantled AMKA
- **Poles**: impregnated wooden poles 10–16 m (strength class II–IV), reinforced-concrete sub-poles PT 4.0–6.0, dismantled reusable sub-poles

### Data gaps

- The stock list has **names only**: no quantities, lengths or prices.
- Volumes of dismantled AMKA, wooden poles and sub-poles are on a separate sheet,
  *"Atkārtotās aprites materiāli"*, which is not included.
- No data on dismantled concrete poles.
- Transport and handling costs must be confirmed with Sadales tīkls specialists;
  otherwise use stated assumptions or modelled examples.
