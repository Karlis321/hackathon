# Otrā dzīve elektrotīkla materiāliem — Second life for power-grid materials

Climathon 2026 | RTU | 30 October – 1 November 2026
Challenge owner: **AS "Sadales tīkls"** (Latvian electricity distribution operator)

## The challenge

> How can we give a second life to cable offcuts and dismantled wooden or concrete
> power-line poles, finding a new, safe and economically viable use that also
> reduces resource consumption and environmental impact?

Teams pick **one** of three material groups:

| Group | Current situation | What's needed |
|---|---|---|
| **A. Cable offcuts** | Offcuts of usable length are already reused in the grid. Very short pieces are not worth it (jointing labour, extra materials, joint reliability). | A use outside the grid, or a way to match offcuts to uses by cable type, length and condition. |
| **B. Wooden poles** | Already reused in power lines (444 poles in 2026 so far). Some cannot be reused. | Uses for whole poles or parts, accounting for impregnation chemicals and their restrictions; safe treatment or recycling routes. |
| **C. Concrete poles** | Not reused: they almost always break and crumble during dismantling. Reinforced-concrete sub-poles *are* reused to extend wooden pole life. | A use for broken poles, parts or crushed material that cuts handling cost and replaces new materials. |

## Expected result

A demonstrable concept or prototype (digital tool, product, logistics or circularity model, business model) showing:

- how the solution works, who it is for and what problem it solves
- practical implementation, including key technical and safety conditions
- a simplified economic case compared with today's material handling
- benefits: resources saved, costs reduced, environmental impact
- next steps for validation or a pilot

The idea must be feasible and its value **backed by numbers**.

### Stock list overview

- **LV indoor/installation copper cable**: (N)YM, H07V-K/U, YSLY, YSLYCY control cable (100 m drums)
- **LV power cable 0.6/1 kV**: NYY-J (Cu), AXMK / AXMK-PE / AXPK-PE (Al, 4×16 to 4×240)
- **MV cable 12/20–24 kV** (mostly Al): AHXAMK-W, AHXCMK-WTC, AXLJ, AXLJ-F TT, AXLJ-TTCL, AXCEL, AXQJ-TT, TSLE
- **Overhead cable**: AMKA in several sizes, plus dismantled AMKA
- **Poles**: impregnated wooden poles 10–16 m (strength class II–IV), reinforced-concrete sub-poles PT 4.0–6.0, dismantled reusable sub-poles

## Data gaps

- The stock list has **names only**: no quantities, lengths or prices.
- Volumes of dismantled AMKA, wooden poles and sub-poles are on a separate sheet,
  *"Atkārtotās aprites materiāli"*, which is not included.
- No data on dismantled concrete poles.
- Transport and handling costs must be confirmed with Sadales tīkls specialists;
